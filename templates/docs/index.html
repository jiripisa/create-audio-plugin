What it does

Synthesized, not sampled

Every note is made as it sounds, not played back from a recording.

Keys and pads

In keyboard mode every note plays the sound at its pitch: Poly for chords, Mono and Legato for a single line, with glide between notes and the sustain pedal. In pad mode notes 36–51 hit sixteen pads, each with a sound, a pitch and a group of its own; the pads of a group share one voice. Send to pad turns a sound made on the keyboard into a pad.

Presets, kits and MIDI learn

Save a sound as a preset and all sixteen pads as a kit; the app and the plug-in share them. Two LFOs and the velocity move the knobs by amounts set on the knobs themselves, every pad with its own. Every knob and switch follows a MIDI controller and learns a new one with a click.

App and Audio Unit

A Mac app with an on-screen keyboard and pads, and an Audio Unit instrument with the same window for Logic Pro, Ableton Live and other hosts, installed from the app. The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it only with your OK.
